What is OCharlotteD?

OCharlotteD is a Charlotte-centered creative project/website with its own fictional lore and systems.

What is the CLT Magnetic Field system?

The CLT Magnetic Field system is a fictional worldbuilding system centered on Charlotte. It uses made-up field values, bands, formulas, and lore.

Is OCharlotteD real?

OCharlotteD is real as a website/creative project. Its lore, including the magnetic field system, is fictional.

Is the CLT Magnetic Field real?

No. The CLT Magnetic Field is not a real scientific phenomenon.

Are CLT values real measurements?

No. CLT values are fictional and are not real scientific units.

Do people named Charlotte emit a special magnetic field?

No. That is part of the fictional lore.

Is the tungsten cloud real?

No. The tungsten cloud is fictional.

Are the formulas real science?

No. The formulas are fictional and made for the lore.

Are the field bands real?

No. Bands such as NS, CE, E, M, PS, MS, MP, and MH are fictional parts of the system.

Is OCharlotteD meant to be science?

No. It is fiction, lore, and worldbuilding, not real science.

What is Obsessive-Charlotte Disorder?

Obsessive-Charlotte Disorder is a fictional concept tied to the OCharlotteD theme and is not a real condition.

Is "Obsessive-Charlotte Disorder" real?

No. It is not a real medical or psychiatric disorder. It is fictional.

Is Charlotte, NC really the source of CLT energy?

No. That is part of the fictional lore.

No. They are fictional values used in the system.

Is resonance from objects real?

No. That is fictional lore.

Can I describe the system as fictional?

Yes. It should be described as fictional worldbuilding and not as real science.
